WHEAT — FLOWERING
Wheat at flowering is a water-sensitive stage. Low soil moisture during this stage warrants closer water-stress monitoring.

WATER MANAGEMENT
Irrigation decisions should consider crop stage, current soil moisture, recent rainfall and expected rainfall together.

SOIL MOISTURE
Soil moisture should not be interpreted as an isolated value. Field condition, crop stage and weather should be considered together.

CROP HEALTH SCREENING
Visible leaf symptoms can support preliminary screening. A field inspection and validated diagnosis are required before disease treatment decisions.

RESPONSIBLE USE
AI recommendations are decision support. Missing or low-quality evidence should reduce confidence rather than be silently invented.
